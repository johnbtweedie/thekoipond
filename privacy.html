Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

The Koi Pond ("we", "us") is a small group of friends who share clips of our own Xbox gameplay. We run a private tool that edits those clips and publishes them to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what that tool accesses and how we handle it. The tool is for our own accounts only; it is not offered to the public.

What we access

  • Our Xbox clips. Clips we recorded on our own Xbox accounts, with basic details such as the game and recording time.
  • Our social accounts. When one of us connects our own YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account, the platform gives the tool permission to read basic account information (such as the account ID and display name) and to publish videos to that account.
  • TikTok specifically. If you sign in with TikTok to connect an account, we receive your TikTok account's basic profile information and permission to post videos to that account, as you approve on TikTok's consent screen. We do not access your messages, followers, other people's content or any information about the people who watch our videos.

How we use it

Only to publish our approved gameplay clips to the connected accounts and to confirm they were posted. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or anything else.

Where it is kept

Access tokens and clip records are stored on our own computer and, for video files, in private cloud storage (Amazon S3) that only we can access. We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone.

How long we keep it

Access tokens are kept while an account stays connected and are deleted when it is disconnected. Clip records and videos are kept for our own archive.

Removing access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions). You can also email us and we will delete any tokens and information we hold for your account.

Children

The tool is used only by its adult operators and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
